# Google Local Guides to TravelQuest XP Conversion

## XP Multipliers

Here's exactly how your Google Local Guides contributions convert to TravelQuest XP:

### Base Contributions (BALANCED)
- **Points**: 0.1 XP per Local Guides point (1000 points = 100 XP)
- **Reviews**: 3 XP per review written
- **Photos**: 1 XP per photo uploaded
- **Videos**: 5 XP per video uploaded
- **Edits**: 2 XP per map edit made
- **Questions**: 3 XP per question answered
- **Facts**: 2 XP per fact added
- **Roads**: 10 XP per road added (rare achievement)
- **Lists**: 8 XP per list created

### Level Bonus
- **Level Bonus**: 200 XP per Local Guides level achieved

## Example Calculation

If you're a Level 4 Local Guides contributor with:
- 1,250 points
- 45 reviews
- 120 photos
- 3 videos
- 15 edits
- 8 questions answered
- 5 facts
- 1 road
- 3 lists

Your TravelQuest XP would be:
```
Base XP:
1,250 points × 0.1 = 125 XP
45 reviews × 3 = 135 XP
120 photos × 1 = 120 XP
3 videos × 5 = 15 XP
15 edits × 2 = 30 XP
8 questions × 3 = 24 XP
5 facts × 2 = 10 XP
1 road × 10 = 10 XP
3 lists × 8 = 24 XP

Level Bonus:
Level 4 × 200 = 800 XP

Total Local Guides XP: 1,293 XP
```

## How It Works in TravelQuest

1. **Connect Your Profile**: Go to Settings → Integrations → Connect Google Local Guides
2. **Automatic Calculation**: System fetches your contributions and calculates XP using the formula above
3. **XP Added to Total**: Your Local Guides XP gets added to your TravelQuest total XP
4. **Level Up**: Combined XP determines your TravelQuest level (1000 XP per level)
5. **Visual Breakdown**: Dashboard shows exactly how much XP comes from TravelQuest vs Local Guides

## Privacy & Updates

- Only public profile data is used
- Data refreshes automatically every 24 hours
- You can disconnect anytime in Settings
- XP conversion is transparent - you see exactly how each contribution adds XP
